04 / Working method

Agent-augmented engineering

AI-augmented delivery.
Human-owned verification.

Coding agents parallelize bounded work. I retain ownership of architecture, acceptance conditions, and final verification.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Problem, constraints, acceptance.

  2. 02

    Parallelize

    Bounded tasks, explicit context.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Code review, tests, evals.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Decisions, trade-offs, operating notes.

The goal is a shorter feedback loop—not outsourcing technical judgment to a model.

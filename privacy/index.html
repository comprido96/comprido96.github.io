Privacy.

What this website collects, why it is needed, and how to exercise your rights.

Last updated · 15 November 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is Federico Gambassi, based in Florence, Italy. For privacy questions or requests, email fedegambassi96@gmail.com.

What is collected

Project enquiries

If you use the contact form, the site collects your name, email address, optional company name, and the message you choose to send.

Technical requests

GitHub Pages processes the technical information needed to serve the website. The pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means the visitor’s browser connects to Google’s servers.

Why the data is used

  • To read, assess, and reply to a project enquiry.
  • To take steps requested before a possible professional engagement.
  • To protect the form and inbox from abuse and spam.

The relevant legal bases are taking steps at your request before entering a contract and the legitimate interest in answering genuine enquiries and securing the service.

Service providers

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Contact-form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to a Google Gmail inbox. FormSubmit may use anti-spam controls, including reCAPTCHA. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own privacy terms.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • FormSubmit Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Retention and sharing

Enquiries are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage any resulting professional relationship, and meet applicable legal obligations. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for up to 30 days. Personal data is not sold or used for mailing lists.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not run its own analytics and does not set first-party marketing cookies. Third-party services used to deliver fonts or protect and process the form may apply their own technical measures.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to the processing of your personal data by emailing the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.
